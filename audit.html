Common Findings

What Most Audits
Uncover

Based on the accounts we've reviewed. These aren't edge cases — they're the norm.

87%

Tracking Errors

Broken or duplicate pixels, misconfigured GA4 events, and missing server-side events that cause Meta to underreport conversions — leading to wasted spend on audiences that are actually converting.

73%

Budget Misallocation

More than half the ad budget sitting in campaigns that haven't driven a single conversion in 30+ days, while top-performing campaigns are budget-constrained.

68%

No Retargeting System

Warm traffic — people who visited the site, viewed products, or engaged with ads — not being retargeted. This is typically the highest-ROAS segment, being left completely untouched.

61%

Ad Creative Fatigue

The same 2–3 creatives running for 60–90+ days with declining CTR and rising CPM, but no testing pipeline in place to refresh and replace underperforming assets.

54%

Scaling Bottlenecks

Campaign structures that can't scale past a certain spend threshold — budget increases that cause CPAs to spike instead of improve, due to audience or structural constraints.

49%

Landing Page Disconnect

A clear message mismatch between ad copy and landing page, causing above-average bounce rates and below-benchmark conversion rates despite decent ad-level CTR.

Questions

Answered.

Is this actually free?
+
Yes — completely. There is no hidden cost, no credit card required, and no obligation to work with us afterward. The audit is a deliverable we provide to demonstrate how we think and what we find. If you want to work together after, great. If not, the findings are yours to act on however you like.
What access do you need?
+
Read-only access to your Meta Business Manager and/or Google Ads account, and Viewer access to your GA4. We never need payment method access, admin controls, or the ability to make changes. Everything we do is observational during the audit phase. We'll send you a step-by-step guide for granting access.
Is there a minimum ad spend to qualify?
+
No hard minimum, but the audit is most valuable if you're actively running campaigns — even at $500–$1,000/month. If you're pre-launch or not yet running ads, we'd suggest booking a free strategy call instead, which we can arrange separately.
How long does the audit call take?
+
The findings call is 30 minutes. We respect your time — we come prepared with a structured presentation of findings prioritised by revenue impact, so we're not wasting time on the call. You can ask questions throughout.
What happens after the audit?
+
You receive a written summary of findings and recommended actions within 24 hours of the call. If there's a fit to work together, we'll present a proposal. There's no pressure, no follow-up sequence, and no hard close. If the audit doesn't demonstrate clear value, we haven't earned the right to ask.
How many spots are available?
+
We take on a maximum of 5 audits per week to ensure quality. Slots are confirmed on a first-come, first-served basis. If we're at capacity, we'll let you know your position in the queue and expected wait time — typically no longer than 7–10 days.
