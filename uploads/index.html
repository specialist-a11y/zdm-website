The Problem

A PDF is not
a proposal.
It's a liability.

Every time you send a static PDF, you hand control of the deal to the prospect. You lose visibility, momentum, and leverage — and you have no idea what happens next.

"While you are sending PDFs, your competitor's proposal is already open on the client's screen."

— PropelDeck, built for this region
  • 01
    You cannot track it

    Did they open it? Which section stopped them? Did they forward it to the actual decision-maker? A PDF tells you nothing.

  • 02
    It cannot close the deal

    No custom pricing, no e-signature, no deposit. The prospect has to leave the proposal, make a call, and remember to come back. Most don't.

  • 03
    It does not travel well

    Download, scroll, print, scan, email back. On a mobile phone. That is the experience you are asking your best prospects to have.

  • 04
    It does not reflect your business

    Caribbean businesses are ambitious, sophisticated, and serious. A flat PDF says otherwise. The deal goes to whoever looks the part.
