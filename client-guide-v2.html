What $200 Gets You

$200/month is a real budget — not a test budget. Used correctly on the right platform with the right offer, it can generate 2–5 qualified leads per month. Here's the honest breakdown of what the money does and what it can't do.

What it CAN do

  • Put your offer in front of 4,000–12,000 targeted people per month
  • Generate 15–40 clicks to a landing page or DM
  • Produce 2–6 inbound enquiries per month (at 10–15% lead rate)
  • Build a warm retargeting audience within 3–4 weeks
  • Test 2–3 ad creatives to find what resonates
  • Create social proof (comments, shares, saves) that compounds

What it CANNOT do

  • Replace organic/referral outreach — this is a supplement, not a solo strategy
  • Generate consistent volume without a strong offer and clear CTA
  • Work without a landing page or response process in place
  • Produce results in the first 7 days — allow 2–3 weeks for data
  • Scale profitably without reinvesting the revenue from first clients

The Non-Negotiable Before You Spend a Dollar

Your offer must be specific. "We do digital marketing" will not convert at $200/month. You need a specific, low-risk offer that removes friction. The best performing offers at this budget level:

  • Free 30-minute ad account audit — low commitment, high perceived value
  • "We'll write your first ad for free" — gets them in the door with a deliverable
  • Fixed-scope starter package ($500 for one campaign setup) — easy yes

Exact Lead Process

This is the step-by-step system for turning $200 in ad spend into paying clients. Follow this in order. Don't skip steps.

Phase 1 — Setup (Days 1–3, $0 spend)

  1. Create your Meta Business Manager account

    Go to business.facebook.com. Set up Business Manager, create an Ad Account, and install the Meta Pixel on your website. This takes 1–2 hours and must be done before you spend a dollar. Without the pixel, you're flying blind.

  2. Build your offer page or lead form

    Decide your entry offer (free audit recommended). Create either: (a) a simple landing page with a Calendly link embedded, or (b) a Meta Instant Form directly inside Ads Manager — no website needed. Option B is faster to launch and typically converts better at low budgets because there's no page-load friction.

  3. Set up your response system

    Every lead that comes in gets a response within 15 minutes. Set up a Zapier automation: New Meta Lead Form submission → Send SMS + Email to yourself → Auto-reply email to the lead confirming their booking. This costs ~$20/month and turns a cold lead warm before they forget they enquired.

  4. Define your targeting audience

    In Meta Ads Manager, create a Saved Audience: Location = your city or region (25–50km radius). Age = 28–55. Interests = "Business owner", "Entrepreneurship", "Small business". Behaviours = "Facebook Page Admins" (this targets people who run a business page — high signal). Estimated audience size: 15,000–80,000. This is your primary cold audience.

Phase 2 — Launch (Days 4–7, spend begins)

  1. Create Campaign 1: Lead Generation ($3/day)

    Objective: Leads. Use your Meta Instant Form. Ad format: Single image or short video (15s max). Headline: "Is Your Ad Spend Actually Working?" or "Free 30-Minute Ad Account Audit — Limited Spots." Body copy: 2–3 sentences maximum. One clear CTA: "Claim Free Audit." Target your Saved Audience from Step 4.

  2. Create Campaign 2: Page Boost ($2.67/day)

    Take your best-performing organic post (highest engagement). Boost it. Objective: Engagement. This is not a lead gen campaign — it's social proof building and audience warming. People who engage here become your retargeting pool. Target same Saved Audience.

  3. Create Campaign 3: Retargeting ($1/day — starts Week 2)

    Custom Audience: Everyone who visited your website, engaged with your Instagram/Facebook, or opened your lead form but didn't submit — last 30 days. Ad: Different creative from Campaign 1. Use social proof angle: "47 business owners have booked their free audit this month." or a testimonial if you have one. This small budget works because the audience is tiny but highly qualified.

Phase 3 — Nurture & Close (Ongoing)

  1. Lead responds → Auto-reply fires within 2 minutes

    Zapier sends them a personalised confirmation email with the Calendly link. Subject line: "Your free ZDM audit is confirmed — here's what to expect." This email briefly explains what happens in the audit call (what you'll review, what they'll walk away with). Sets expectations, builds anticipation.

  2. 24 hours before call → reminder SMS

    Use Zapier + Twilio or a Calendly reminder. Simple text: "Hey [Name] — quick reminder your ZDM audit is tomorrow at [TIME]. Reply RESCHEDULE if you need to move it. See you then." This reduces no-shows from ~40% to ~15%.

  3. Discovery/audit call → use the 10-question framework

    This is your sales call disguised as a service call. You're auditing their situation, identifying their real problem, and positioning ZDM as the solution. Run the BANT+ framework from Section 03 of the main guide. Aim to close or get a clear next step in the same call.

  4. Same-day proposal email

    Within 2 hours of the call, send a one-page proposal email. Not a PDF — a clean plain-text or HTML email with: the problem you heard, the solution you'd implement, the investment (Starter $1,500 or a fixed starter package), and one clear CTA: "Reply YES to move forward and I'll send the agreement today." Speed is the differentiator here.

  5. Follow-up sequence if no reply (3 touches)

    Day 2: "Just checking you got this — any questions?" Day 5: "Still have one spot available for [month]. Let me know." Day 10: "Closing out this enquiry — is now not the right time, or did something change?" After Day 10, move them to a monthly newsletter nurture and don't chase further.

Free Channels to Stack

The $200 boost plan works fastest when paid is layered on top of free organic activity. These channels cost only time and compound over months. Pick 2–3 that fit your natural style and be consistent.

Highest ROI

LinkedIn Organic

Post 5 days/week. Format: short text posts (no links in post — put them in comments). Content: what you learned from running ads, a client problem you solved, an industry myth you're debunking. The LinkedIn algorithm heavily rewards consistency. Expected reach per post at 500 connections: 500–3,000 organically.

Time: 30 min/day  |  Payoff: 4–8 weeks

Visual brand

Instagram Organic

Post 4–5 times/week using your existing SVG post designs. Stories daily (use Higgsfield clips). The goal at this stage is social proof — when a lead looks you up, they see an active, credible brand. Hashtags: mix broad (#digitalmarketing) with niche (#metaads #performancemarketing) and local (#[yourcity]marketing).

Time: 1 hr/day  |  Payoff: 6–10 weeks

Direct pipeline

LinkedIn DM Outreach

Send 20 personalised connection requests per day to your ICP. Filter: Business owner / Founder / Director + industry + location. Connection message (300 chars): "Hey [Name] — I noticed you're running [business type] in [city]. I work with businesses like yours on paid media. No pitch, just thought it was worth connecting." When they accept, wait 48hrs, then offer value — not a sales pitch.

Time: 30 min/day  |  Payoff: 1–3 weeks

Local network

Facebook Groups

Join 5 local business owner groups and 3 industry-specific groups. Don't pitch. Answer questions about ads, tracking, and marketing when they come up — naturally. Add genuine value 3–4 times/week. After 2–3 weeks of value, you can mention your free audit when directly relevant. This builds trust that ads can't buy.

Time: 20 min/day  |  Payoff: 3–6 weeks

Long-term compounding

Cold Email

Use Apollo.io free tier (50 emails/month) to find e-commerce brand owners and send personalised outreach. Best subject line at this budget: "Quick question about your [brand] ads." Body: one specific observation about their business, a question, and your offer. Keep to 5 sentences max. Expected reply rate: 3–8% with strong personalisation.

Time: 1 hr/day  |  Payoff: 2–4 weeks

Trust multiplier

Google Business Profile

Set up a free Google Business Profile for ZDM INC. Anyone who Googles you after seeing an ad will see a credible listing. Ask your first 2–3 clients for a Google review immediately after onboarding. Even 3–5 real reviews dramatically increase trust and can surface you in "marketing agency near me" searches for free.

Time: 1hr setup  |  Payoff: Ongoing passive

Scaling Beyond $200

The $200 plan is designed to generate your first 2–4 clients. Once you have those clients and a repeatable system, scale ad spend using a reinvestment formula — not by guessing.

The Reinvestment Rule

Reinvest 20% of new client retainer revenue into ads

Your first client at $1,500/month → add $300 to ad budget ($500 total).
Two clients at $1,500/month → add $600 to ad budget ($800 total).
Three clients at $1,500/month → $200 + $900 = $1,100/month ad spend.

At $1,100/month you can run proper campaigns on Meta AND LinkedIn. You now have proof, testimonials, and case studies to use in your ads. This is when growth compounds.

When to Hire vs. Keep Solo

Stay solo until...

  • You have 5+ active clients
  • Delivery is taking more than 30 hours/week
  • You're turning down qualified leads
  • Revenue is consistently $8,000+/month

First hire should be...

  • A junior media buyer / VA — handles daily optimisation and reporting (not strategy). $800–$1,500/month part-time.
  • This frees you to focus on sales and strategy — the highest-value activities.
  • Use Upwork, Contra, or your own LinkedIn network to find them.
