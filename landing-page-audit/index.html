What we actually check

Five passes over your page.

Not a checklist score out of a hundred. A person going through your page in order, the way your customer does, saying what breaks and why.

First impression

02

Before you ask

The questions we get.

Is it actually free, or free until the invoice arrives?

Actually free. You get the recording and the written findings with no invoice attached. We do this because roughly one in five people who see what is wrong asks us to fix it, and that is a better way to earn work than cold calling.

Will someone call me and pitch?

No. The audit arrives by email and you can ignore it. If you want to talk after watching, reply and we will book a time. Nobody rings your phone because you filled in a form.

I do not have a landing page, just a website. Does that count?

Yes. Send whichever page your ads, posts, or link in bio point to. That is the page doing the work, and it is usually the homepage, which is part of the problem we would talk about.

My site is on Wix, Shopify, or WordPress. Does that matter?

Not for the audit. The findings are about clarity, friction, proof, and measurement, and those apply on any platform. Where a fix depends on your platform, we say so.

Why only fifty?

Because each audit is a real person spending a couple of hours on your page, and fifty is what we can do properly alongside client work. When the fifty are gone the audit becomes a paid engagement. We are not going to quietly reset the counter and run the same offer again next month.

What happens if I am number fifty one?

You will be told straight away rather than left waiting. We will hold your details only if you ask us to, and let you know what the paid version costs so you can decide with a real number in front of you.

What is this tracking snippet, and do I have to use it?

It is a few lines of JavaScript that go in your site header, the same kind your analytics already uses. It records how visitors move through the page, not who they are, and we can hand you the removal step the day you want it gone. It is optional. Skip it and you still get the walkthrough and the ranked fix list, which is the bulk of the value.

How much traffic counts as enough?

As a rough line, a few hundred visits to that page over two to three weeks. Below that the recordings still make interesting viewing, but you would be making decisions off too few people. We will tell you honestly which side of that line you are on rather than dressing up thin data.

What do you do with my details?

We email you the audit. That is it. No list is sold, no partner is passed your address, and one reply saying stop means we stop.
