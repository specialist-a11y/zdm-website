Our Story

Crafted with Heart,
Built for the Island.

"Every stitch tells a story of the sea, the sun, and the people who carry it."

Island Tote was born from a simple love — beautiful, functional bags that celebrate where we're from. Each piece is handcrafted in Barbados using quality materials built to last far beyond a single season.

We believe what you carry should carry a story. And ours starts right here, on this island.

Why Island Tote

More Than a Bag.

Handcrafted with Care
Every bag is made by hand using quality stitching and materials chosen to stand the test of sun, sand, and sea.
Island-Born & Proud
Designed and made in Barbados — a brand rooted in Caribbean culture, colour, and community.
Built to Last
No fast fashion here. Island Tote bags are made to be used, loved, and passed on — season after season.
