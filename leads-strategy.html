Days to First Results

The ZDM lead generation strategy uses the AI Audit Tool as the primary lead magnet. Instead of cold-pitching services, we lead with value — a free, instant audit of a prospect's ad account. This removes friction, demonstrates expertise before any sales conversation, and filters for businesses that are actively running ads and care about performance.

The strategy operates on two parallel tracks:

Organic Track

  • Content that educates and attracts inbound leads
  • LinkedIn and Instagram as primary platforms
  • Direct outreach to warm prospects
  • SEO targeting high-intent audit keywords
  • Referral system from existing clients

Cost: Time only. Results build over 60–90 days and compound.

Paid Track

  • Meta ads driving to the free audit tool
  • Google Search ads for high-intent keywords
  • LinkedIn ads targeting marketing decision-makers
  • Retargeting visitors who didn't convert
  • Lookalike audiences from existing clients

Budget: $500–$1,500/mo to start. Scalable once CAC is proven.

Who We Are Targeting

Primary

E-Commerce Owners

DTC brands spending $5K–$50K/mo on Meta or Google. Frustrated with rising CPAs and inconsistent ROAS.

  • Shopify / WooCommerce stores
  • $1M–$20M annual revenue
  • Have run ads for 6+ months
Secondary

B2B Service Businesses

Agencies, SaaS, coaches, and consultants running lead gen ads. Want more qualified leads at a lower CPL.

  • LinkedIn + Meta primary channels
  • $500K–$10M annual revenue
  • Sales team in place
Tertiary

Marketing Managers

In-house marketers who manage ad budgets but lack deep performance expertise. Want to show ROI to their leadership.

  • $10K–$100K monthly budgets
  • Mid-to-large companies
  • Need an external audit to justify agency hire
02

Lead Funnel

Every lead follows the same path — from first touch to retained client. The audit tool sits at the centre of the funnel, converting interest into a qualified conversation.

1
Awareness
They See ZDM Content or Ad
A pain-point post on LinkedIn, a Meta ad targeting business owners, a Google search for "Facebook ads audit" — any channel that puts ZDM in front of someone running paid ads.
2
Interest
They Land on zdminc.com
The website CTA drives them to the free audit sign-up page. The offer is clear: "Find out exactly what's wrong with your ads — free, in 60 seconds."
3
Conversion
They Create an Account & Run the Audit
They sign up via Supabase, input their ad data, and receive a full AI-generated audit report. This is the moment they experience ZDM's value — before paying anything.
4
Qualification
They Request an Account Manager or Book a Call
After seeing the audit, they either book a strategy call via the calendar link in the report or request a managed service. Both trigger a ZDM team notification.
5
Close
Discovery Call → Proposal → Retained Client
The audit becomes the foundation of the discovery call. The prospect already knows what's wrong — ZDM presents the solution and the retainer is signed.

Why the Audit Tool Changes the Sales Dynamic

Traditional agency sales starts with "trust us." The audit tool starts with evidence. A prospect who has already seen a detailed breakdown of their ad account problems is 3–5× more likely to sign than one who received a cold pitch. The audit does the qualification and the selling simultaneously.

05

Audit Tool as Lead Magnet

The audit tool is not just a product — it is the most powerful lead generation asset ZDM has. Every person who runs an audit has self-qualified: they run paid ads, they care about performance, and they're willing to spend 5 minutes telling us exactly what their account looks like.

What the Audit Tool Does for Lead Gen

  • Captures email on sign-up (Supabase account = lead in your database)
  • Reveals the prospect's ad spend, platforms, and pain points
  • Delivers enough value to earn a follow-up conversation
  • Creates urgency — they've just seen their account problems in writing
  • Positions ZDM as the expert before any sales call

Post-Audit Follow-Up Sequence

  1. Immediate: Audit results page with "Book a Call" CTA and "Request Account Manager" option
  2. 24 hrs: Email from ZDM — "Here's what we found in your account + 3 quick wins"
  3. Day 3: Email — case study from a client with similar issues
  4. Day 7: Email — limited slots for managed service, personalised offer
  5. Day 14: Final follow-up — "Still seeing these issues?" with direct calendar link

Optimising the Audit Sign-Up Page

Headline

Lead with the outcome, not the tool:

"Find out exactly what's killing your ad performance — free, in 60 seconds."

Not: "Try our AI audit tool"

Social Proof

Add 2–3 short testimonials from clients who ran the audit and booked:

"Found $8K in wasted spend in the first audit. Booked a call the same day." — E-commerce founder

Friction Removal

Make the sign-up as fast as possible:

• "No credit card" badge
• "Free forever" badge
• "Takes 5 minutes" label
• Google/Apple sign-in (future)

08

30-Day Launch Plan

The first 30 days focus on organic — build the content rhythm, start outreach, and gather early audit sign-ups. Paid ads launch in Week 3 once the landing page is tested and the follow-up sequence is in place.

Week 1 — Foundation

  • Optimise LinkedIn and Instagram profiles — bio, banner, audit link
  • Write and schedule 4 LinkedIn posts
  • Write and schedule 3 Instagram posts + daily stories
  • Set up email follow-up sequence (5 emails)
  • Start 10 LinkedIn connection requests per day
  • Identify 50 target prospects for outreach list

Week 2 — Outreach Begins

  • Begin LinkedIn DMs to Week 1 connections
  • Post consistently — 4× LinkedIn, 3× Instagram
  • Engage on 15 posts per day (comments)
  • Follow up on Day 1 DMs with no response
  • Goal: first 5 audit sign-ups

Week 3 — Paid Ads Launch

  • Launch Meta pain-point campaign ($15/day)
  • Launch retargeting campaign ($10/day)
  • A/B test 2 ad creatives per campaign
  • Continue organic — now 2 tracks running simultaneously
  • Goal: 10 audit sign-ups total

Week 4 — Optimise & Close

  • Review paid ad CPLs — kill underperforming creatives
  • Follow up with all audit sign-ups who haven't booked
  • Book first discovery calls from organic outreach
  • Launch Google Search ads if budget allows
  • Goal: 2–3 discovery calls, 1 closed client

The Math at Scale

20 audit sign-ups → 6 complete the audit and engage → 4 book a call → 1–2 become clients. At an average retainer of $3,000/month, acquiring 2 clients per month from $1,500 in ad spend = 4× return on ad spend from the lead gen budget alone, before accounting for organic leads which cost nothing.

Scale the ad budget when the CAC is proven. Do not scale before the follow-up sequence and sales process is converting consistently.
